This encounter first appears as a familiar reflective problem: a transition did not work, and the teacher later identifies pacing as the issue. Through Kolb, Gibbs, or Schön, the event could be organized as observation, evaluation, and future adjustment. Yet in Tauira this encounter should not remain a closed lesson about technique.

Rhizomatically, the event links to recurring nodes across the project: analytic performance, material orientation, visible evidence, and the production of compliance. The encounter therefore matters less as a single failure of pacing than as one expression of a wider pattern in which pedagogical rigour, classroom objects, and evaluative pressure repeatedly thin relation. See also [[enc-2025-T1-W4-L1]] for the recurrence of visible evidence and institutional legitimacy, [[enc-2025-T1-W5-L3]] for the recurrence of affective shifts and role instability, and [[memo-001]] for the diffractive map of compliance-affect across encounters.

The worksheet-compliance pattern visible here resurfaces in [[enc-2025-T1-W5-L3]] and is theorised through Barad in [[memo-001]] and [[memo-003]]. The question of what counts as visible learning links this encounter to [[enc-2025-T1-W4-L1]] and its analysis in [[memo-002]].
