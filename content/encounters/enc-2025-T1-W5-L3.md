S3 arrived before the bell. I watched them rearrange a chair near the window and then, after a moment, replace it exactly as it was. This small act of anticipatory arrangement stayed with me throughout the lesson.

The blank whiteboard held the room in a different way from the previous two encounters. No instruction, no deadline — just space. When I asked what students remembered from W4, S3 spoke immediately: *"Everything connects to everything and nothing connects to anything."* The room laughed. See [[essay-001]] for the theoretical reading of this moment.

The *theory-laughter bridge* — the connection between [[Rhizomatic Thinking]] and collective affect — crystallised in this encounter. For the accepted gap suggestion identifying the blank whiteboard as an unnamed material participant, see [[memo-003]].
