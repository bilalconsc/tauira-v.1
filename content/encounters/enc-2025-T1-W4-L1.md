Three students were absent. Their absence structured this lesson as much as those present — the room felt different, thinner. The [[Assessment Rubric]] from W3 was still on several desks, a material residue of the previous week's constraint.

The introduction of [[Rhizomatic Thinking]] through Deleuze and Guattari produced a slow, cautious engagement. I moved table-by-table, asking each group their entry point. The rearranged tables — less clearly oriented toward any centre — afforded this differently from the standard arc configuration.

This was the first lesson I explicitly theorised the table arrangement. See [[memo-002]] for gap analysis and [[essay-001]] for the theoretical synthesis across all three encounters.
