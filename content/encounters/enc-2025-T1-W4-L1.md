This encounter can easily be narrated in reflective discourse as a successful act of differentiation. Kolb, Gibbs, and Schön all support that reading by helping the teacher convert the event into professional learning about flexibility. In Tauira, however, the event should remain open as a site where medium, legitimacy, and authority are redistributed.

Rhizomatically, this encounter connects to other nodes beyond differentiation: written legitimacy, assessment comfort, visible rigour, and the politics of medium. It is therefore not only about one student and one task. It belongs to a wider network in which writing repeatedly appears as the default carrier of seriousness, while alternative media expose the material-discursive preferences through which teacher authority is stabilised. See [[enc-2025-T1-W3-L2]] for the recurrence of visible evidence, and  [[memo-002]] for the cross-encounter map of legitimacy, medium, and comfort.
