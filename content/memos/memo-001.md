This memo responds to [[enc-2025-T1-W3-L2]], the encounter in which the worksheet-compliance and relational-thinning clusters became analytically visible. The compliance pattern traced here recurs in [[enc-2025-T1-W5-L3]] and the affective dimension connects to the legitimacy disturbance mapped in [[memo-002]].

## Gap: Time Pressure
The encounter identifies the worksheet, desks, analytic sequencing, and my desire for visible thinking, but the temporal pressure of the lesson is still not fully drawn into the assemblage. The need to complete, evidence, and move onward may have shaped the room before the slowing became visible. What looked like a weak transition may therefore have been mediated by the felt authority of lesson time itself.
A further gap lies in the creative task. I marked the move from conceptual understanding to creative design as the point where the room slowed, but I did not yet ask what kind of uncertainty the creative task introduced into an environment already organised around analytic clarity and correctness. In Tauira, this matters because the creative task may recur elsewhere as a threshold where pedagogical control weakens and other relations become possible. This threshold logic surfaces more fully in [[enc-2025-T1-W5-L3]] and [[memo-003]].

## Diffractive Reading 1 — Barad

Read through Barad, the event loses the simplicity of teacher action followed by student response. The slowing was not mine, theirs, or the task's alone. It emerged through the intra-action of worksheet, desks, bodies, sequencing, completion pressure, and my own orientation toward visible evidence. No single element stands outside the event as its master cause.

This matters for rhizomatic analysis because the scene can now connect outward to other Tauira nodes: visible evidence, analytic performance, compliance, and pedagogical tempo. The encounter becomes less a lesson about pacing and more a map of how rigour, material arrangement, and institutional timing repeatedly produce relational thinning. That same Baradian reading is applied to legitimacy in [[memo-002]] and to the concept-affect threshold in [[memo-003]].

## Diffractive Reading 2 — Massumi

Read through Massumi, the drop in energy can be understood as a shift in affective intensity rather than only a decline in engagement. The room did not stop working; it changed register. Curiosity thinned, compliance thickened, and bodies remained with the task without fully remaining with one another.

This reframes the encounter productively. Instead of asking only how I should improve the transition next time, Tauira allows the scene to be read as one instance in a wider affective pattern where institutional forms redirect intensity away from relation and toward evidence. That outward link is what makes the encounter rhizomatic rather than merely reflective. The same affective redistribution is traced in [[enc-2025-T1-W4-L1]] through the lens of medium and recognition, and in [[enc-2025-T1-W5-L3]] through the concept-sadness threshold.
