This memo responds to [[enc-2025-T1-W3-L2]], the first encounter in which the *table-deadline-joke cluster* became legible as an assemblage.

## Gap: The Groundskeeper

The scene description notes the sound of mowing outside the window. I accepted the gap suggestion that this non-human participant — the groundskeeper, the mower, the sound — mediated the room's attention threshold before S3's joke. It produced a low-frequency auditory presence that the classroom was already navigating. S3's joke cut across that texture. The assemblage analysis should name this participant.

## Diffractive Reading 1 — Barad

Read through Barad's (2007) agential realism, the laughter event loses its agent (S3) and becomes an intra-action: the body, the sound, the rubric's authority, and the spatial arrangement of round tables all co-constituted the event. No single element caused the laughter.

## Diffractive Reading 2 — Massumi

The 8-second pause between the laughter and my next utterance becomes, via Massumi, a productive interval — not a failure to manage but a moment in which affect circulated freely. The pause was pedagogical even if unintentional.
