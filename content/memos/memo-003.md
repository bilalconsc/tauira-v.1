This memo responds to [[enc-2025-T1-W5-L3]], the lesson in which the blank whiteboard structured the opening in ways I had not fully anticipated.

## Gap: The Blank Whiteboard (Accepted)

I accept this gap suggestion. The blank whiteboard is a Material participant that has not appeared as a node in the graph. Its deliberate emptiness — a choice I made before students arrived — produced a different kind of opening. The room could not orient toward instruction because there was none to orient toward. This created a condition in which S3's joke became structurally necessary, filling the gap that the blank board had opened.

Adding node-007 (Blank Whiteboard) with a `mediates` edge to Boredom (node-005) and Laughter (node-006) would make the rhizomatic map more accurate for this encounter.

## Diffractive Reading 1 — Barad

The blank whiteboard was not passive. It participated. Its emptiness intra-acted with student bodies — those who looked at it expectantly, those who looked away, those (like S3) who read it as an invitation.

## Diffractive Reading 2 — Rancière

The withheld instruction redistributed what Rancière calls the *partage du sensible* — the distribution of the sensible. Students became producers rather than receivers. S3's joke was not disruption; it was emancipated spectatorship finding its form.
